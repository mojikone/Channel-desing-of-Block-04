Block 04 channel terrain (200-year design), 2 m cells, EPSG:32640.
Block04_channel_surfaces_2m.tif: the designed channel surfaces (bed and side slopes) of the 37 channels, the lowest surface where channels meet; nodata elsewhere.
Block04_terrain_with_channels_2m.tif: the ground of the 0.5 m project terrain model resampled to 2 m over the channel corridors (bed, embankments, access roads and 100 m around), with the designed channels cut in; nodata outside.
Per channel: volumes/<channel>/channel_terrain.tif (designed surface) and ground_2m.tif (the 0.5 m ground on the same grid).
