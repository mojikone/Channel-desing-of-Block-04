Block 04 designed-channel terrain, revision R9 (200-year design), 2026-10-12
One GeoTIFF per channel: <channel>/channel_terrain.tif, EPSG:32640 (WGS 84 / UTM 40N), 2 m cells, float32, NoData -9999.
Each raster is the designed channel surface stamped into the ground: flat bed at the designed invert, 1:1.5 side slopes,
cut daylighting at 1:1.5 or a 4 m crest embankment in fill, from the reach sections of the R9 design on the 5 m DEM
(NSA_Batinah_5m_Block_04.tif). Cells outside the channel footprint are NoData. Built with the Channel Terrain tool.
Volumes against the DEM are in channel_quantities.csv (cut, fill, footprint, riprap surface, corridor, land take, cost).
